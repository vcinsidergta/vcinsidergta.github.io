Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy covers the Vice City Insider website and the "VCInsider Publisher" tools (the "Apps") that Vice City Insider uses to publish its own videos to its own accounts on YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok through their official APIs.

What the Apps do

The Apps upload short videos created by Vice City Insider to accounts owned by Vice City Insider, and read basic statistics (such as view counts) of those videos so we can see which videos work. They do not post on behalf of anyone else.

Data we collect

  • Our own account data: access tokens for our own accounts, stored only on our own computer, used only to publish and read statistics for our own videos.
  • Public statistics: public, aggregate information such as viewer counts of live streams in the GTA VI category, obtained from official platform APIs.
  • Website visitors: this website does not use cookies, analytics or trackers and does not collect personal data. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (see GitHub's privacy statement).

We do not collect, sell or share personal data of TikTok, Instagram, Facebook or YouTube users, and we do not use the Apps for advertising.

E-mail

If you e-mail us (for example a creator replying to a permission request), we keep the conversation only to record the answer. Reply "stop" and we will not contact you again. Ask us and we delete it.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are deleted when we disconnect an account. To request deletion of any data relating to you, e-mail vcinsidergta@gmail.com; we answer within 30 days.

Children

Our services are not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We will post any changes on this page with a new date.
